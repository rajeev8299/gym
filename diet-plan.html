Quick diet tips

Diet follow karne ke golden rules

Protein pehle

Har meal mein protein rakho (eggs, paneer, chicken, dal). Yeh muscle banata hai aur pet bharata hai.

Paani piyo

Roz 3-4 litre paani. Khali pet subah 1 glass garam paani nimbu ke saath piyo - metabolism badhega.

Meal timing

Breakfast 8 baje, lunch 1 baje, snack 5 baje, dinner 8 baje - fix time pe khao. Late night mat khao.

Chewing slow

Khane ko 20-30 baar chew karo. Pet ka signal brain ko 20 minute mein pahuchta hai - jaldi mat khao.

No sugar drinks

Cold drink, packaged juice, energy drinks sab band. Nimbu paani, chaas, coconut water best hai.

Sleep matters

7-8 ghante neend lo. Kam neend se weight gain aur muscle loss hota hai. Phone raat ko door rakho.

Oil control

Kam tel aur ghee use karo. Ghee 1-2 spoon tak theek hai. Deep fried food bilkul avoid karo.

Consistency

Ek din cheat meal chalega par poora din junk mat khao. 80% diet + 20% workout = 100% results.

Sabziyaan khao

Roz 2-3 katori sabzi khao. Palak, lauki, tori, karela, gobhi, matar - seasonal sab best hai.
